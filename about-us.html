Who We Are

Allegiance Partners was built to be small on purpose — a tight core of two partners and a bench of independent specialists, brought in only when their expertise is actually needed.

We don't have offices. We don't carry staff, overhead, or the layers of process that come with them — because none of that has ever been what actually protects or grows a family's wealth. What does is senior-level expertise, delivered directly, by people who have spent decades doing exactly this work at the highest levels and have no further interest in doing it inside a large institution.

That's the whole model. No junior analysts learning on your file, no committee — just the right person, at the right moment, doing work they've already done at scale for thirty years.

The Founders

Equal partners in the firm

John Alexander
Co-Founder & Principal
Before Allegiance Partners, John was CEO of Lloyds Private Bank Switzerland, where he ran operations spanning Gibraltar to Dubai — a mandate that put him inside the private banking practice of half a dozen jurisdictions, and as many regulatory regimes, at once. He describes himself, only half-jokingly, as "the Ibn Battuta of international private banking" — well-travelled enough to have seen how every version of the private bank actually works, and just as often, how it doesn't.
Harold Hyunho Lee
Co-Founder & Principal
Harold is John's equal partner in the firm. A CPA by training, he spent years at KPMG before moving into industry as CFO for two US multinationals operating across Asia and Australia, and later as CFO of H Mart and of Western Beef. He went on to lead the Southpole Family Office and Foundation in Fort Lee, NJ, as Head of Family Office and Chief Investment Officer, and is also a Certified Fraud Examiner. That combination — public accounting discipline, operating-company CFO experience, and single-family-office leadership — gives him deep relationships and expertise across the Korean-American ultra-high-net-worth community and cross-border planning between the US and Asia.

How We Work

 

For most of our founder clients — the more straightforward, single-jurisdiction engagements — you're working directly with John and Ja'Nae, start to finish. For the more complex, multi-jurisdictional, multi-generational engagements, we bring in the full bench: the right specialist, for the right piece, at the right time.
We are, collectively, old enough to have made most of the mistakes already, expert enough that we don't need training wheels, and independent enough that we only take on work we actually find interesting. That combination is rare in this industry. We think it's also exactly what a family with real complexity actually needs.
