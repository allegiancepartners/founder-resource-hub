Roadmap
Phase-by-Phase Plan
Discover, Prepare, and Decide — the milestones that take this business from today to close.
This is a live, interactive tool — every business's road map is built around its own financials, industry, and exit timeline. Click into any step below to see the level of detail a real workspace would include.
Built on the CEPA® Value Acceleration Methodology™ — three phases (Discover, Prepare, Decide) applied across the "Three Legs of the Stool": Business readiness, Personal readiness, and Financial readiness. Most owners under-invest in the Prepare phase — it is usually where the largest, cheapest gains in sale price are found.

Baseline valuation & Value Builder ScoreValue Driver

Confirm the owner's post-exit "number"Personal
What does the business need to net you, after taxes and deal costs, to fund the next chapter? This anchors the whole plan and belongs in the Personal leg of the stool, not an afterthought at closing.
2
Year 1 · Q3 – Year 2 · Q1
Prepare — Business Readiness
0/5 complete
Reduce customer concentrationValue Driver
No single customer or channel should be able to sink the business if it walks — buyers price this risk directly into the multiple.
Document SOPs and key processesValue Driver
Move recipes, vendor relationships, and day-to-day operating knowledge out of the owner's head and into written systems a new owner can actually run from.
Build management bench strengthValue Driver
Identify and develop at least one person who could run day-to-day operations for an extended stretch without the owner — directly targets the Hub & Spoke driver.
Formalize customer and vendor contractsValue Driver
Move key relationships from handshake/informal to written agreements with reasonable terms — this alone often moves the Switzerland Structure and Recurring Revenue drivers.
Move financials to CPA-reviewedFinancial
Buyers and lenders discount unaudited, internally-compiled statements. A reviewed (or eventually audited) financial history removes a common diligence objection before it's raised.
3
Year 1 · Q3 – Year 2 · Q1
Prepare — Personal & Financial Readiness
0/3 complete
Personal financial planPersonal
Model living expenses and goals against the estimated net-of-tax sale proceeds — confirming the plan actually funds retirement or the next venture before a deal is signed, not after.
Basic estate planLegal/Deal
Will, Power of Attorney, and Healthcare Proxy — often overlooked by owner-operators focused entirely on the business side of the sale.
Coordinate tax structuring ahead of saleFinancial
Entity structure, installment-sale treatment, and any trust/gifting strategy are far more flexible before a Letter of Intent than after — engage CPA/counsel early.
4
Year 2
Decide — Go to Market
0/5 complete
Engage M&A advisor / business brokerLegal/Deal
Runs a structured process — buyer identification, confidential outreach, and competitive tension — rather than a single-buyer negotiation.
Formal Quality of Earnings reportFinancial
An independent QoE report, commissioned before buyers ask for one, shortens diligence and materially strengthens negotiating position.
Prepare Confidential Information MemorandumValue Driver
The document that tells the business's story to qualified buyers — built on the cleaned-up financials and documented systems from the Prepare phase.
Buyer management presentationsValue Driver
With management bench strength in place, the owner is no longer the only person who can credibly present the business to a buyer.
Negotiate Letter of IntentLegal/Deal
Price, structure (asset vs. stock sale), earnout terms, and transition-period expectations all get set here — with M&A counsel and the advisor at the table.

Close & Transition

How long, and in what capacity, the owner stays involved post-closing — balancing buyer comfort against a clean personal exit.
ClosingFinancial

Value Drivers
The 8 Drivers of Value
What buyers actually pay a premium for, independent of the raw financials.
The Value Builder System's 8 Drivers of Value are the factors buyers actually pay a premium for — independent of how good the underlying financials look. Score each driver 1 (weak) to 10 (strong) to see how it moves the overall Value Builder Score and, on the Valuation Estimator tab, the effective multiple a buyer is likely to pay.
