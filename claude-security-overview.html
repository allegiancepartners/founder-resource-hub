The Security of Your Information When We Use Claude

A note for clients and referral partners on how AI-assisted drafting and analysis is handled at our firm.

Allegiance Partners uses Claude, an AI assistant built by Anthropic, to help us prepare scenario analyses, research, and planning materials more efficiently. Because that work often touches your confidential information, we want to be direct about exactly how it is handled — and what we are still responsible for.

  1. Your information is not used to train Claude

    Allegiance Partners uses Claude under Anthropic's commercial (business) terms, not a free consumer product. Under those terms, Anthropic is contractually prohibited from using anything we submit — including names, financial details, or planning scenarios — to train its models. This is a contractual commitment on Anthropic's part, not a setting that depends on how we configure the tool.

  2. Your information stays inside a controlled environment — we control what leaves it

    When we work with Claude, your information is processed in an isolated, access-controlled session and is not published, indexed, or made visible to anyone outside our firm as a result of that processing. Nothing leaves that session unless Allegiance Partners chooses to send it — the same as any other document on our computers. If you receive something we prepared with Claude's help, it reached you because we emailed it to you or placed it in a shared folder, not because Claude distributed it.

  3. Backed by independent security standards

    Anthropic's infrastructure is independently audited to SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 standards, and Anthropic supports customers' GDPR and CCPA compliance obligations. Allegiance Partners uses Claude's Team plan, which includes centralized firm administration and single sign-on.

    More advanced controls used by regulated enterprises — audit logging, custom data-retention policies, HIPAA-ready configurations — are part of Anthropic's Enterprise tier, which we are not currently on; we're happy to revisit that if a client's own compliance requirements call for it. Current certification details are published at Anthropic's Trust Center (trust.anthropic.com).

  4. Our obligations to you don't change

    Using Claude as a drafting and research tool does not change Allegiance Partners' fiduciary and confidentiality obligations to you. We remain fully responsible for how your information is used, stored, and shared — exactly as if the work had been done entirely by hand.

This summary reflects Anthropic's published commercial terms and security documentation as of September 2026 and is provided for general information only. It is not a warranty, and not a legal or compliance opinion — please consult your own counsel with any specific questions. Current details can always be confirmed directly at Anthropic's Trust Center (trust.anthropic.com).
